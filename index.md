\# Privacy Notice — Data We Hold About Your Business



Apex Automations collects publicly available business information about UK 

tradespeople and construction-trade companies, for the purpose of providing 

verified business-contact data to other companies operating in the trades 

supply chain (e.g. suppliers, insurers, and service providers).



\*\*What we collect:\*\* business name, trade category, registered address/town, 

publicly listed phone number and website (where available), and Companies 

House registration details (company number, status) for incorporated 

businesses.



\*\*Where it comes from:\*\* OpenStreetMap (an open, publicly licensed mapping 

database) and Companies House (the UK's official, publicly accessible 

company register), plus publicly available business websites.



\*\*Why we hold it:\*\* to compile and sell business-contact data packets to 

companies who supply goods or services to the trades sector.



\*\*Your rights:\*\* if you are a sole trader or otherwise an identifiable 

individual under this notice, you have the right to object to this processing 

or request your details be removed from our records. To do so, contact us at 

'contact.apexautomations@proton.me'.



\*\*We do not\*\* sell or share personal data for any purpose other than the 

business-contact use described above, and we maintain a suppression list to 

honour all removal requests permanently.
